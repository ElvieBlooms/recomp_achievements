# Notices

## RetroAchievements metadata

The achievement titles, descriptions, point values, author credits and
display order in `recomp_achievements/catalog/` come from
[RetroAchievements](https://retroachievements.org) and are credited to
RetroAchievements and each achievement's author. RetroAchievements'
[terms](https://retroachievements.org/terms) permit third parties to
reference and reuse achievement titles, descriptions and related metadata.

- Achievement **trigger logic** belongs to RetroAchievements and is **not**
  used. Every unlock check in this mod was written independently against
  Gen1Recomp's own save data and events.
- No RetroAchievements **badge artwork** is included.
- This mod is an **unofficial fan project, inspired by RetroAchievements and
  not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by RetroAchievements**.
  Unlocks are local to your game. They are never sent to RetroAchievements
  and don't count toward any RetroAchievements account, rank or leaderboard.

## Gen 2 game facts

`recomp_achievements/data/gen2_*.lua` (event-flag numbers, map trainers,
items and gifts) is generated by `tools/gen2_data.py` from the
[pret/pokegold](https://github.com/pret/pokegold) and
[pret/pokecrystal](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal) disassembly
projects. It holds names and numbers only, no game code or assets.

## Gen 3 game facts

`recomp_achievements/data/gen3_frlg.lua` is generated the same way by
`tools/gen3_data.py` from the
[pret/pokefirered](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered) disassembly
project: names and numbers only.
