# Differences from vanilla

Recomp Achievements reads the game; it doesn't change how any of it plays.

- **START menu:** one extra row, **TROPHIES** (**TROPHY** in Gen 1 and
  Gen 2, so the box keeps its original width). Turn **START MENU ENTRY**
  off in the mod's options to leave the START menu as the game has it, and
  open the menu from **OPEN TROPHIES** in the Mods manager instead.
- **Popups:** unlock banners and small progress cards drawn over the game.
  **UNLOCK POPUPS**, **PROGRESS POPUPS** and **UNLOCK SOUND** turn them off.
- **Save file:** the mod keeps its records under `modData.recomp_achievements`.
  The game's own save data is never written.

Nothing else differs: no species, moves, items, maps, scripts, battles or
music are touched.
