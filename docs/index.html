Extra Credit is a second, just-for-fun board for this mod's bonus achievements and achievements added by other mods. It has a combined total and a board for each mod. Submit it from the Extra Credit page (SELECT, then RIGHT) or with /extra on Discord.

To submit: in the game open START > TROPHIES, press SELECT, and copy the card into the form, or send it to the Discord bot with /score. Only achievements from the RetroAchievements lists count; bonus and mod achievements don't. On equal points, the shorter play time (when the score was reached) ranks higher.

Scores are self-reported (honor system). The check code only catches typos. See a name that shouldn't be here? Use Report on its row.

An unofficial fan project, not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to RetroAchievements. Scores here don't count on retroachievements.org. Achievement lists are by RetroAchievements and their authors.
